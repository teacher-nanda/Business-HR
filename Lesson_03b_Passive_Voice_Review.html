📘 Passive Voice · Explanation

Active vs. Passive Voice

Same meaning, different focus.
In the passive, the thing that receives the action comes first.

Colour code: main verb / past participle verb to be other helping verb (has, will, should, had, would)

🔷 Active: the doer comes first

I should correct this data.
The manager showed the presentation to us.
Mom bought a gift.

🔶 Passive: the receiver comes first

This data should be corrected.
The presentation was shown to us (by the manager).
A gift was bought.
Passive voice = verb to be + past participle
The verb to be shows the tense. The main verb is always the past participle.
Why use the passive?
Yesterday I went to the mall. My phone was stolen.
= Someone stole my phone. I don't know who, and it doesn't matter. What matters is my phone.

📘 Passive Voice · Explanation

How to Form the Passive

Keep the same tense as the active sentence.
Put the verb to be in that tense, then add the past participle.

Colour code: main verb / past participle verb to be other helping verb (has, will, should, had, would)
TenseActivePassive
Present simpleI do the dishes every day.The dishes are done every day.
Past simpleThe manager showed the presentation to us.The presentation was shown to us.
Present perfectThe painter has finished my house.My house has been finished.
Future simpleI will organize this mess.This mess will be organized.
Modal (should)I should correct this data.This data should be corrected.
Formula: object of the active sentence → subject of the passive + be (in the same tense) + past participle
Watch the irregular participles: show → shown, steal → stolen, do → done, buy → bought.

📘 Passive Voice · Explanation

When Can We Use the Passive?

We need a doer and a receiver. In the passive, the receiver becomes the subject.

Colour code: main verb / past participle verb to be other helping verb (has, will, should, had, would)

✅ One doer + one receiver

Nanda killed her plants.
The plants were killed.
"By who?" I don't know! Or I don't want to say. The passive lets us leave the doer out.

❌ No receiver = no passive

My plants died.
My friends went to the park.
Die and go have no receiver, so we can't say "My plants were died". But to kill = kill something, so it can be passive.
Present perfect: who did it?
My manager has postponed our meeting. = There's no more meeting, and I know who did it.
Our meeting has been postponed (by my manager). = There's no more meeting. The result matters more than the doer.
"by + doer" is optional. Leave it out when the doer is unknown, obvious, not important, or you don't want to say.
Mom bought a gift. → A gift was bought.

🔁 Reported Speech · Review

Reported Speech: Quick Recap

Reported speech tells us what someone said.
The tense usually steps one step back in time.

TenseWhat they saidReported
present simple"I work from home."She said she worked from home.
present continuous"I am working on it."He said he was working on it.
present perfect"I have finished."She said she had finished.
will"We will call you."They said they would call me.
can"I can't come."He said he couldn't come.

🕐 Time words change too

today → that day
tomorrow → the next day / the following day
yesterday → the day before
next week → the following week

🗣 said / told / asked

She said (that) she was busy.
She told me (that) she was busy.
She asked where the office was. (no inversion)
Don't mix them up!
Passive: the focus is the receiver. "The report was sent."
Reported speech: someone said something. "She said she sent the report."
Ask yourself: is there a speaker (said, told, asked)? If yes, it's reported speech.

🔁 Reported Speech · Review

Passive Voice or Reported Speech?

Both can use past forms like was and had been, so they look similar. Ask two questions.

Colour code: main verb / past participle verb to be other helping verb (has, will, should, had, would)

🔶 Passive voice

Is there a speaker (said, told, asked)? No.
Is there be + past participle? Yes.
The offer has been accepted.
This data should be corrected.

🔷 Reported speech

Is there a speaker (said, told, asked)? Yes.
Did the tense step back (is → was, will → would)? Yes.
She said she sent the report yesterday.
He told me they would call back.
Careful: was alone does not make a passive. "She asked me where the office was" is reported speech, because was is just the verb to be and there is no past participle.

🔀 Both Together

Passive Voice Inside Reported Speech

Yes, you can have both in one sentence!
Step the verb to be back one tense. Keep the past participle.

Colour code: main verb / past participle verb to be other helping verb (has, will, should, had, would)
What they said (passive)ReportedThe change
"The meeting has been postponed."He said the meeting had been postponed.has been → had been
"The report will be finished tomorrow."She said the report would be finished the next day.will be → would be
"My phone was stolen."He told me his phone had been stolen.was → had been
"The data is being checked."She said the data was being checked.is being → was being
Bonus: "It is said that…" / "It was announced that…"
We use these when the speaker is unknown or not important.
It was announced that the office would close early.
It is said that the CEO will resign.
Check it: reported speech (said / told) + passive (be + past participle) in the same sentence.
The passive part is only the verb group: had been postponed.
